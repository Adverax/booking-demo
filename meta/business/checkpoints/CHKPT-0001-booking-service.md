## What we're building

A small backend service that lets a business publish when its resources (a specialist,
a room, a desk) are available and lets customers book and cancel 30-minute slots
without double bookings. The studio owns the frontend and the client relationship;
this service is reached only through its HTTP API, behind the studio's gateway.

## Key trade-offs

- No authentication: anyone who knows a booking id can cancel it; identity is the
  studio gateway's concern.
- One idempotency key is unique across the whole service, so a retry is always safe
  and unambiguous.
- Fixed 30-minute slots, UTC only, no holidays or per-date exceptions.
- Deferred: waitlist (candidate in the backlog).

## Risks & open questions

- Concurrent bookings of the same slot must resolve to exactly one winner; this drives
  the storage and locking design.
- With no authentication, a leaked booking id lets a third party cancel it.
